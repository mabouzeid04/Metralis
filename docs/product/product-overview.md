# Product Overview

## 1. Summary

This product is an **AI-first maintenance and reliability platform** built for factories.  
It has two core layers:

1. **A modern CMMS**  
   A clean, fast, user-friendly system for logging issues, repairs, and machine history.  
   Fully usable without AI.

2. **An AI Maintenance Co-Pilot**  
   A diagnostic and documentation assistant that:
   - analyzes symptoms  
   - reads machine history  
   - interprets manuals  
   - recommends likely causes  
   - guides technicians step-by-step  
   - auto-creates and auto-closes work orders based on conversation  

Together, these form the **Factory Intelligence Layer**:  
a long-term, structured memory of every failure and fix, enabling smarter diagnostics over time and laying the foundation for future autonomous manufacturing.

---

## 2. What Problem We're Solving

Factories—especially in emerging markets—run on:

- aging machines  
- informal troubleshooting  
- undocumented tribal knowledge  
- WhatsApp messages  
- technicians solving the same problem over and over  
- inconsistent repair logs  
- inaccessible manuals  
- slow, error-prone maintenance processes  

Legacy CMMS tools exist, but they fail because:

- they are admin systems, not diagnostic systems  
- they require heavy manual data entry  
- they don’t actually reduce downtime  
- technicians avoid using them  
- they provide no intelligence or recommendations  

Factories want **less downtime**, **faster diagnosis**, **better documentation**, and **a way to preserve knowledge**.

No existing tool solves this well.

---

## 3. What the Product Is

### A. **A Modern CMMS (Core System)**

A clean, minimal CMMS designed to be used daily:

- machine and asset registry  
- work orders (create, assign, close)  
- symptom and failure logging  
- repair logging  
- parts and inventory basics  
- manuals and SOP management  
- machine history timeline  
- dashboards (failures, MTTR, parts usage)  
- mobile-first design  

It’s a system technicians and managers can use even without AI—simple, fast, and operationally useful.

---

### B. **AI Maintenance Co-Pilot (Intelligence Layer)**

This is the differentiator.

The co-pilot:

- interprets symptoms  
- compares with past incidents  
- identifies recurring failure patterns  
- pulls relevant manual sections  
- ranks likely root causes  
- suggests diagnostic steps  
- writes work orders and repair logs  
- explains machine failures in plain language  
- assists with training and onboarding  

Effectively, it becomes the **expert engineer who knows every machine’s full history**.

---

### C. **Factory Intelligence Layer (Strategic Layer)**

As more data flows into the system, the AI builds:

- a knowledge graph of symptoms → causes → fixes  
- per-machine longitudinal “health profiles”  
- cross-factory failure patterns (when allowed)  
- part-failure correlations  
- technician performance insights  
- anomaly detection  

This layer gets stronger over time and becomes the base for:

- predictive maintenance  
- OEM integrations  
- automated root cause analysis  
- semi-autonomous troubleshooting  
- fully autonomous maintenance workflows (long-term vision)

1. Factories are losing massive money to downtime.  
2. There is no system that combines **CMMS + AI diagnostics** seamlessly.  
3. Technicians hate data entry; AI makes documentation painless.  
4. Manuals are unreadable; AI makes them actionable.  
5. Tribal knowledge disappears when staff leave.  
6. Emerging markets rely heavily on older machines that benefit most from AI context.  
7. Factories want autonomy, but foundational data layers are missing.

This product provides the missing link between today’s chaotic maintenance workflows and the future of autonomous plants.
